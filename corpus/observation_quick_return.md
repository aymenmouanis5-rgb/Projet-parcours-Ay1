What Davor of Tilque saw after the storm summer

Three years ago the storms kept most boats ashore for the best part of a summer. I had a loan to pay, so I cursed every cloud. But when we went out again the following season, I was astonished. The nets came up heavy, and fish were feeding close to the Tilque shore where nobody had seen them for years.

The lake answered a single quiet summer faster than I would ever have guessed. I made no plan for it, and neither did anyone else. The weather simply gave the water a rest, and the water took it.

I do not know what to make of this. Some say it proves the lake is stronger than the old stories claim. Others say it proves that resting matters more than we thought. It may be that both are right. All I can tell you is what came up in my nets.
