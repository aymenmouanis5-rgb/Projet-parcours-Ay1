What Sefa of Hennad noticed in her nets

I keep a knotted cord for each season, one knot for each good fish, as my mother did. Laying the last few cords side by side, I do not like what I see. The nets are not empty, and I would not claim that they are. But the fish in them are smaller than they were, and I have to go further from shore to find the old ones.

The deep channel off the far point used to hold big fish all summer. This year I hauled there three times and came back with little I would have kept as a girl.

Perhaps it is the warm water. Perhaps it is only my stretch of the lake. I cannot say what it means for the valley, and I am no elder. I only say that a lake can look well from the shore long after it has begun to weaken.
