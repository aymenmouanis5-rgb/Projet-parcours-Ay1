Tilque market account, last season

The busiest stalls in the valley again. Boats from Tilque went out early and came home heavy, and traders paid good coin through the warm months. When too many boats landed in the same week the price sagged, and when the weather kept them ashore it jumped back. Those who sold late in the season did best.

Three boat loans were paid down, though none was cleared. One family gave up its boat to its lender and now rents it back by the season. Others say they could finish paying within a few good years, if the good years last.

The clerk writes that debt does not wait for the lake. A fisher who owes money must land fish this season, whatever anyone says about the next one. Nobody on the quay disputes it, and nobody on the quay enjoys it.
