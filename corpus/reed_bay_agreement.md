The Reed Bay Understanding

Twenty-three years ago, after a run of poor springs, the fishers of the four hamlets shook hands at the elder's house and agreed to leave Reed Bay alone during the spawning weeks. Nothing was written down, and no one was charged with watching over it. Each boat simply kept its word.

For seven years the understanding held, and many believed the springs improved because of it. Others pointed out that the springs had improved in places nobody had rested.

It ended without a quarrel. Newcomers arrived who had never shaken anyone's hand. A hard year came, and one Tilque boat worked the bay out of need. Nobody said anything. The next spring there were three boats, and by the spring after, no one remembered who had been first. Old fishers still argue over whether the understanding saved the bay, or only cost families good fishing for seven years.
